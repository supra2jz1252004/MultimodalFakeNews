# Locked protocol and methodology

Protocol SHA-256: `1a961fd33503d16b52a70a01b83e37b2b426bd1bde7a116500dddc9e04131eb0`. It was locally locked before VERITE encoder/model inference, followed by a source hash lock. This is a local pre-inference protocol lock, not public preregistration. No test labels selected thresholds, bank entries, hyperparameters or models. All current official available rows were retained without replacement.

Mapping: `true → 1 (truthful)`; `miscaptioned/out-of-context → 0 (misleading pair)`. Fakeddit's distant-supervision fake/real labels differ. All accuracy, Macro-F1 and classifier AUC/AP are expressly cross-dataset, cross-task **transfer diagnostics**. The original argmax decision is retained, tie→class0. Classifier probability of Fakeddit real is interpreted diagnostically as probability of truthful, without external calibration. Positive class for ROC-AUC/AP is truthful; AP is average precision, not trapezoidal PR area. Loss probabilities are clipped to [1e-6,1−1e-6].

Frozen A/B use original BERT CLS 768 and ImageNet ResNet50 2048 features; B also uses raw CLIP cosine. BRAS C uses two margins; BRAS D uses cosine plus both margins. Original head checkpoints, per-feature training normalizers and the 4,096-row Fakeddit training bank remain frozen. k=16 and `(m_text+m_image)/2` were fixed. Query matches by text, byte/pixel content, group or pair identifiers are excluded from reference neighborhoods. CCR's stored gate and policy are actually loaded/executed; fallback B is an identity control.

Actual authorized images and captions were encoded using the project's pinned **CLIP ViT-B/32**, not the authors' supplied ViT-L/14 features. Original BERT revision, CLIP revision, ImageNet1K_V2 weights, 64-token CLS contract, 77-token CLIP limit, EXIF first-frame RGB handling, image transforms and float32 normalization were verified. Extraction loads encoders sequentially on CPU, 6 threads, batches of 16, hash-bound 64-row chunks. No feature fitting, fine-tuning or retraining occurs.

Primary classifier hypotheses: seed 42 B−A and D−B Macro-F1, Holm-corrected together. Other seeds and C−B/CCR−B are a separate secondary family. 5,000 common-component bootstrap resamples (seed 20261109) yield percentile 95% CIs. 10,000 within-component swaps of complete model prediction vectors (seed 20261110) recompute Macro-F1; two-sided Monte Carlo p-values use +1 correction. Repeated rows are never treated as independent McNemar trials. Seeds share data and are not pooled as independent replications; their mean/sample SD is descriptive.

Alignment is a separate threshold-free task. Higher cosine/margin should rank the verified true pair above its misleading counterpart; exact ties receive half credit. Primary BRAS-mean minus cosine ranking comparisons are tested separately for miscaptioned and out-of-context pairs, with Holm correction across those two tests. Four directional-score comparisons form a secondary family. Strict and 1e-6-tolerance ties, family-only uncertainty, source/category breakdown and known-overlap exclusion are predeclared sensitivities. Correspondence AUC/AP use original rows, avoiding duplicate counting of a true record across the two pairing tasks.
