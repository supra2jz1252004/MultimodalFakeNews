# Prior-work comparison and evidence audit

**Search cutoff:** 6 November 2026. This is a focused review of the eleven works named in the task, not a systematic review of the entire field. No absence-of-prior-art or novelty claim follows from this list. A short review of the accessible source used for each work is recorded below; where the publisher page blocked full-text access, claims are restricted to its abstract or indexed excerpts.

## Implications for the proposed extension

1. CLIP similarity, similarity-guided fusion, semantic mismatch, bidirectional alignment, prototype banks, reliability gates, and controlled ablation are all established ideas in this area.
2. The closest conceptual evidence against equating similarity with truth is MCSF; the closest same-dataset architecture is CMCM; the closest controlled manipulation studies include VERITE/CHASMA and MEEL; and the closest broader evaluation design is the 2026 large-scale empirical study.
3. The narrow candidate distinction investigated here is an explicit pair-relative inference feature: for each direction, subtract the mean top-*k* valid similarity to a fixed, source-family-aware, training-only bank of actual examples. Bidirectional in-batch similarity, contrastive queues, and positive-negative similarity margins already exist. Whether this fixed-bank feature is sufficiently distinct from them requires further literature review, especially the inaccessible ViLID and full MCSF/CMCM sources.
4. This project measures binary Fakeddit label prediction. Neither high alignment nor a synthetic nonmatching replacement establishes factual truth. The labels are distant-supervision categories, and some fake categories can be highly aligned.

## Additional targeted prior-art search

These works were found because the requested method explicitly uses bidirectional similarity and reference negatives. They further constrain the gap beyond the initial eleven references.

| Work | Verified overlap and relevance | Source access |
|---|---|---|
| **Multi-Modal Fake News Detection via Bridging the Gap between Modals** (Entropy 2023) | Reports no significant correlation between CLIP image-text similarity and authenticity in its analysis, and argues that abstract images and complex correspondence make a single similarity score an unreliable truth test. It uses image descriptions and entity/global features for fusion. | Full MDPI paper PDF reviewed. [Paper](https://doi.org/10.3390/e25040614) |
| **Multimodal Fake News Detection: MFND Dataset and Shallow-Deep Multitask Learning** (IJCAI 2025) | Uses symmetric in-batch text-to-image and image-to-text similarity, a momentum encoder, and queues of recent cross-modal embeddings to soften negative-pair treatment. This is close prior art for bidirectional sample-reference comparisons, although it is a trainable contrastive objective, not a fixed per-example top-*k* residual feature at inference. | Full IJCAI paper PDF reviewed. [Paper](https://www.ijcai.org/proceedings/2025/0891.pdf) |
| **A simple contrastive embedding framework for low-resource fake news detection** (2025) | Publisher-indexed text describes top-*k* retrieval consistency filtering and filtering triplets by a positive-minus-negative similarity threshold. This is a related reference-relative margin in a text/claim and contrastive-sample setting; it makes a broad claim that top-*k* negatives or similarity margins are new untenable. | Abstract and indexed method excerpts only; full publisher page was inaccessible. ## Label-order note

The six category names in the paper text are not a numeric order. This repository's official `6_way_label` encoding is `0=True`, `1=Satire/Parody`, `2=False Connection`, `3=Imposter Content`, `4=Manipulated Content`, and `5=Misleading Content`. The analysis uses this verified numeric mapping and reports the human-readable names. The task's prose list order must not be used as an integer encoding.
