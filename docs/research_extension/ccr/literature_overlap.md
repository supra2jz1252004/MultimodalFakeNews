Review date: 6 November 2026. This is a targeted equivalence check, not an exhaustive novelty search. Evidence grades distinguish accessible full methods from abstracts or indexed excerpts. Publisher claims are not independently reproduced here. No source establishes CCR's superiority.

## Claim boundary

The precise study contribution is an auditable, bounded comparison of per-example loss-based selection between two existing frozen-feature heads. It includes train-only OOF fitting, known-family partitions, source-feature exclusions, validation-only decisions, proper scoring rules, a random control, and preserved negative results. These support reproducibility and a focused empirical question. They do not establish algorithmic novelty, causal identification of CLIP's effect, generalization, factual verification, or publication eligibility.

The strongest conceptual overlap is SSPC's cross-fitted utility gate; the strongest mathematical correction to BRAS is the CSLS identity. COOLANT++ and MCSF require full-paper verification before manuscript claims about exact differences. No SOTA table is defensible across incompatible datasets, splits, task definitions and architectures.

## Search record

Sources were opened at their official arXiv, ACL Anthology, publisher, repository or dataset pages. Targeted searches included exact MCSF title/DOI, COOLANT++ concept-bank gating, cross-fitted utility gates, and original CSLS. Search failure or inaccessible text is recorded above rather than interpreted as absence of prior art. No outside paper's reported score is used as a directly comparable baseline in this experiment.
